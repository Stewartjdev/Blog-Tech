## Projector
The image below encapsulates the main circuit board, protected by a silver metal covering. This shield guards sensitive circuitry against unwanted electromagnetic interference (EMI), helping the device operate reliably by reducing disturbances caused by electromagnetic signals.

On the right side, the green square connected to the circuit board by a thin black cable appears to be a wireless antenna. Wi-Fi serves as an invisible bridge between devices and networks, allowing information to travel without a physical connection. Through this communication, devices can exchange data and access information across the internet.
